# Cybersecurity Ethics

This file defines the ethical rules I follow while learning cybersecurity, ethical hacking and penetration testing.

## Main Principle

Cybersecurity skills must be used responsibly, legally and ethically.

Any testing, scanning or experimentation must only be performed in environments where I have permission.

## What I Will Do

I will practice only in:

- My own devices and lab environments
- Intentionally vulnerable machines
- Platforms designed for cybersecurity learning
- Academic environments where testing is allowed
- Systems where I have explicit authorization

## What I Will Not Do

I will not:

- Scan or test public systems without permission
- Attempt to access accounts, services or data that are not mine
- Share private information, credentials or sensitive data
- Use cybersecurity knowledge to cause harm
- Run tools against networks where I do not have authorization

## Learning Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to document my learning process and build skills for defensive security, ethical hacking and penetration testing in a responsible way.

## Notes

These rules may be updated as I learn more about professional cybersecurity standards and legal requirements.
